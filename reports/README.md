# Generated evaluation reports

Run `make evaluate` after full training to generate `metrics.json`, or `make smoke`
after downloading PathMNIST to generate `smoke-metrics.json`.

Working reports in this directory are ignored until their provenance and results
have been reviewed. The first reviewed Kaggle report is versioned as
[`baseline/metrics.json`](baseline/metrics.json); see its
[provenance and reproduction notes](baseline/README.md).

No placeholder scores or results from synthetic fixtures are published here.
